Glassbridge / storyboard

Look at it. Ask about it. Hear the answer.

Smart glasses stream what you are looking at to Claude, and Claude answers in your ear a few seconds later. No phone comes out of your pocket.

Capture Ray-Ban Meta glasses Relay iPhone over Wi-Fi Brains FastAPI on a MacBook Round trip 4 to 13 seconds

One loop, five stages

Every interaction runs the same path. The glasses and phone only capture and play back. Everything expensive happens on the Mac, which is why the laptop has to be on the same network.

01Capture One JPEG frame plus 5s of audio, grabbed in parallel
02Transcribe faster-whisper, distil-large-v3, on CPU
03Think Claude reads the frame and the question, may search the web
04Speak ElevenLabs streams MP3 back as it is generated
05Play Audio routes to the glasses over Bluetooth
Runs on the glasses and phone Runs on the MacBook

That is an M6 by 1.0. The bin on your left is M6 by 1.25, so the threads will bind. You want the bin one row down.

You pick from the right bin and leave. Under ten seconds, and your hands never stopped holding the bolt.

The same loop elsewhere

Nothing changes but the question

Every scenario below runs the identical pipeline. What varies is the sentence you say and the thing you happen to be looking at, which is the whole argument for putting the camera on your face.

At the table, abroad

Translate this.

The menu is in front of you and the waiter is waiting. You get natural phrasing read aloud, not a literal word swap held under a phone.

Hands covered in flour

What do I do next?

Claude sees the pan, reads the state of the onions, and picks up from the recipe you asked about two turns ago.

Someone's kitchen windowsill

Am I underwatering this?

Vision identifies the species, a web search fills in the care details, and the answer comes back the moment you noticed the drooping leaves.

Small print on a box

Read this to me.

Text gets reflowed into something speakable and the fine print is skipped unless you ask for it. Both hands stay free.

Paying at the counter

Log this.

Vendor, total, and category get read back for correction on the spot, instead of a shoebox of receipts three weeks later.

Walking past a shelf

Save that.

A one-line note about the thing you just looked at, written to a scratchpad you can search later. Effortless capture is the entire point.

Honest status

What actually runs today

Working

The whole loop, on the iPhone

Point the phone at something, tap, and hear Claude answer. Capture, transcription, vision, and voice all work end to end at the times quoted above.

Blocked

The glasses as the camera

Written and verified against a mock device, but two Meta-side bugs block real hardware. Their developer signup rejects the account, and developer mode registers the app without ever showing a camera permission toggle.

Working

The glasses as mic and speaker

Audio never touches the blocked SDK. Turn on Use for Calls in Bluetooth settings and the glasses carry your voice and Claude's reply today.

Next

Hands off the phone entirely

Wake word and rolling visual context are built but not wired in. Spoken start and stop, silence detection, and audio cues are what turn this from an app into an assistant.
