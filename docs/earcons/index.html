Earcon options

Three complete sets. Listen on the glasses if you can, or anything small and tinny — they are designed for a speaker inches from an open microphone, and laptop speakers flatter them.

What they are designed against. Every cue is under 400ms, because a sound that outlasts the thing it announces is a delay. All of them sit between roughly 300 Hz and 2 kHz: below that the glasses' HFP link rolls them off, above that they read as an alarm. The two pairs that must never be confused are heard you / dropped it and got the shot / still working, so each set inverts the contour rather than only moving the pitch. Still working repeats every 1.6s and is deliberately the quietest thing here.

Glassset a

Pure sine intervals, currently shipping. Clean and neutral, closest to iOS system sounds. Reads as 'a device acknowledged you'.

Still workingcontinuous

Replaces the repeating tick. These loop seamlessly and play under the whole wait, fading in after 1.2s and out when the reply starts. Quiet, low, and deliberately unlike speech, so they do not fight the wake recogniser that is still listening underneath. Let one run for a few seconds to hear the loop point, or rather to not hear it.
